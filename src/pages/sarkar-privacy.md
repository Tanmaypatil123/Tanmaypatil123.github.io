_Last updated: November 2026_

This policy covers **Sarkar**, a turn-based election strategy game for Android, developed by Tanmay
Patil (Elytron AI). It explains what information the game collects, why, and how you can delete it.
Sarkar is a work of fiction: its parties and leaders are invented, and the game does not ask about or
record your own political views.

## Information we collect

**Account and identity.** Sarkar uses Unity Authentication. When you start, you play as a guest, which
creates an anonymous player ID. You may save the account with a username and password so you can sign
in on another phone. Your password is handled by Unity's authentication service. We never see or
store it. Sarkar does not ask for your email address, phone number or real name.

**Profile and game progress.** Your display name, username, coins, gems, cosmetics, settings, wins,
match count, best result and match history are stored against your player ID in our backend
(Supabase) so your progress is backed up and syncs across devices. Your display name, wins and best
result appear on the in-game leaderboard.

**Online matches.** When you play online, your display name, chosen leader and moves in that match
are stored so every player in the room sees the same game.

**Purchases.** The in-game shop keeps a log of what you bought with coins or gems (and, once real-money
purchases are connected, with money) for support. Real-money payments are processed by Google Play.
We never receive your card or bank details.

**Local-only data.** Sound, music, vibration and language settings are also kept on your device.

## What we do not collect

Sarkar does not show ads, does not use the advertising ID, does not use third-party analytics, and does
not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. We do not sell your data or share it
with advertisers.

## Who processes your data

- **Unity Technologies** (Unity Authentication) for sign-in.
- **Supabase** for storing your profile, progress and online matches.
- **Google Play** for downloads and payments.

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

## Children's privacy

Sarkar is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information,
contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

## Data deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the game: **Settings → Account → Delete account**, then tap
again to confirm. This removes your Sarkar profile, progress, match history and purchase log from our
backend, deletes your Unity Authentication account, and clears saved data on that device. You can also
[request deletion without the app](/sarkar-delete-account).
