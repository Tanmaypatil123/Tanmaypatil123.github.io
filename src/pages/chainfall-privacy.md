_Last updated: October 2026_

This policy covers **Chainfall**, a mobile Chain Reaction game for iOS and Android. It explains what
information the game collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

## Information we collect

**Account and identity.** Chainfall uses Unity Authentication. By default you play as a guest, which
creates an anonymous player ID stored only on your device. You may optionally link a Unity Player
Account (email or social sign-in through Unity's own sign-in flow) to keep your progress across
devices. We never see or store your sign-in password — that is handled entirely by Unity's
authentication service.

**Gameplay and progression data.** Your trophies, level, XP, coins, diamonds, match history, quest
progress, season standing, and cosmetic collection are stored against your player ID in our backend
(Supabase) so your progress syncs across devices and ranked results can be verified fairly.

**Advertising identifiers.** Chainfall shows optional rewarded video ads through Unity LevelPlay
(ironSource mediation) in exchange for in-game coins or diamonds. On iOS, the game asks for App
Tracking Transparency permission before requesting your advertising identifier (IDFA); you can
decline this and still watch ads, just without personalized targeting. On Android, the mediation SDK
may use the Android advertising ID under the same conditions. We do not control how individual ad
networks mediated through LevelPlay use this data beyond showing you ads — see Unity's and
ironSource's own privacy documentation for details on their mediation partners.

**Analytics.** We use first-party analytics to understand gameplay patterns (for example, which modes
are played, session length, and crash/error reporting) so we can improve the game. This data is tied
to your anonymous player ID, not to your real-world identity.

**Local-only data.** Local notification reminders, audio and display settings, and the one-time
tutorial state are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

## What we do not collect

Chainfall does not require your real name, phone number, or physical address to play. The Shop and
in-app purchases are currently preview-only and non-functional — no payment information is collected
or processed by Chainfall at this time.

## Children's privacy

Chainfall is not directed at children and is configured to report itself as a non-child-directed app
to its ad mediation partners (COPPA = No). If you believe a child has provided us with personal
information, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

## Data deletion

You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account**. This
removes your Unity Authentication account and your synced progress from our backend, and clears saved
data on that device. If you'd like to confirm deletion or request removal of data we may still hold
(for example, in backups or fraud-prevention logs), email us and we'll handle it directly.
